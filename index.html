    How to use Valtrack

    A quick guide and answers to common questions.

    Getting started

    1. Enter your username and tag, for example lev0nt and 101, and press Track.
    2. Press the refresh icon to load your recent matches, rank and rank history. The app also refreshes by itself when you open it, at most every 10 minutes.
    3. Tap the star to save a player as a favorite. Favorites let you switch profile quickly under Account, and you can compare ranks with them in one tap.
    4. Use the moon or sun icon to switch between light and dark mode.

    Around the app

    • Overview: win rate, record, K/D, ACS, current and peak rank, today's session and your recent form.
    • Matches: every match you have fetched. Press Details to see all players, damage, first bloods and who dominated who. Select a player there to open their profile or save them as a favorite.
    • Stats: win rate and K/D per agent and map, the players you team up with, and your weapons: which you use most, which gets you the most kills per round, and which kills you most. It also shows attack and defence, economy, opening duels and trades, clutches and ability use.
    • Progress: trend charts (hover or tap a dot for details), accuracy over time, rank history, a tilt check and a rank goal estimate.
    • Compare: see how far you are from another player's rank and roughly how many games it takes to catch up.
    • The Game mode selector at the top applies to every tab. It starts on Competitive.

    On a phone, swipe left or right anywhere on the page to change tab.

    FAQ

    Why do I only see my last 20 matches?

    The data source only returns your most recent matches. Valtrack keeps every match it has fetched, so your history grows each time you open the app. Open it regularly, and use Export under Account now and then as a backup.

    Why does the refresh button sometimes not fetch anything?

    To stay within the data source's limits, fetches are spaced out: automatic refreshes wait 10 minutes and the button waits one minute. The icon still spins briefly, and the status line tells you when you last fetched. If the data source asks us to slow down, the app pauses for a moment.

    Where is my data stored?

    Only in this browser, on this device. It doesn't appear on other devices, and clearing your browser data removes it. Use Export and Import under Account to back up or move it.

    Can I see live matches?

    No. There is no public live data for Valorant. Matches show up after they have finished, the next time you fetch.

    What do ACS, ADR, HS%, FB and FD mean?

    ACS is your average combat score per round, ADR your average damage per round, and HS% the share of your shots that hit the head. FB and FD are first bloods and first deaths: who got the first kill, or died first, in a round.

    How are the rank goal and catch-up estimates calculated?

    Valtrack replays the climb 1,500 times. Each game is a win with the win rate shown, worth one of the wins you actually got in your recent ranked games, or a loss worth one of your usual losses. With no history it assumes about 14 to 26 RR per game. You see the median number of games and how many games cover 80% of the runs. It is still an estimate: hidden rating, win streaks and demotion protection are not modelled, and Immortal and Radiant are approximated.

    What happens when I open another player's profile?

    Valtrack loads their matches and rank the same way as yours, and saves them in this browser. Use the "Back to" link above the name, or the Account menu, to return. While you look at them, Compare and Rank goal treat them as the main player.

    How are the weapon statistics calculated?

    Kills and deaths per weapon come from the kill feed of each match. Rounds, damage per round (ADR) and headshot percentage are counted in the rounds where you bought that weapon, so a gun you picked up mid-round is only partly included. Kills per round is the quick measure of impact. Weapon data is saved for matches fetched with this version, so press refresh to load your latest ones.

    How are sides, economy and opening duels worked out?

    Sides come from who planted or defused the spike in each round, since the sides swap after round 12. Buy types come from your team's average loadout value at the start of the round. Opening duels use the first kill of each round, and a trade is a kill on your killer within 5 seconds. Matches where the data is missing are left out. Press refresh to load these for your latest matches.

    How are clutches, ability use and the tilt check worked out?

    A clutch starts when you are the last one alive on your team against at least one enemy, and counts as won if you survive and your team takes the round. Ability use is the number of casts per round for each agent, and the rounds won with little or lots of utility when the data has it per round. The tilt check compares your win rate after a win with your win rate after a loss within the same session, and by game number and time of day, using the matches you have saved. Small samples can mislead.

    Why are older matches missing player details?

    Player details are only saved for matches fetched with the latest version, and the data source only returns recent matches. Press refresh to fill in your latest ones.

    How are draws counted?

    A tied score is shown as a draw. It counts as neither a win nor a loss.

    Can I install Valtrack as an app?

    Yes, when you open it from a web address or localhost. Use your browser's Install or Add to Home Screen option. It then opens like an app and shows your saved data offline.

    Saved on this device

    Your favorites, tracked accounts and match history are saved in this browser, on this device only. They won't appear on other devices, and clearing your browser data will remove them. Use Export to keep a backup.
